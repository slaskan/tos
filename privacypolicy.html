Privacy Policy

This application ("the App") is a private automation tool used solely by its owner.

The App does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The App uses TikTok's official API to authenticate and upload videos.

The only data processed is:

  • OAuth tokens provided by TikTok
  • Video files selected by the owner

No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party data collection occurs.

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device and are never transmitted to any external server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the owner of the App.
